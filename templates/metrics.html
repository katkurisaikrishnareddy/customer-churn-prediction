Phase 1

Exploratory Data Analysis & Churn Drivers

🎯 Target Class Distribution & Imbalance (7,043 Accounts)

Dataset Baseline
Key Finding: Churn baseline is 26.5% (1,869 accounts) vs 73.5% (5,174 accounts) who stayed. Because of this ~2.8 : 1 imbalance, model evaluation emphasizes PR-AUC and Recall over naive overall accuracy.

📊 Churn Rate Across Key Categorical Features

Bivariate Analysis
Key Finding: Month-to-month contracts experience a massive 42.7% churn rate (vs 2.8% on 2-year contracts). Fiber optic subscribers churn at 41.9% (vs 18.9% on DSL), largely driven by high pricing and lack of technical support. Electronic check billing shows 45.3% churn vs ~15% for automatic bank/card payments.

📈 Continuous Feature Distributions

Numeric KDE
Key Finding: Churn peaks during the first 0–6 months of tenure. Customers paying over $70/month have significantly elevated cancellation probability.

🗺️ Feature Correlation Heatmap

Multicollinearity
Key Finding: TotalCharges is highly correlated with Tenure (0.83). Regularized linear and tree-based pipelines effectively manage this relationship.
Phase 2

Model Training, Selection & Imbalance Ablation

🤖 5-Fold CV Model Comparison & One-SE Rule

Model Selection
Selection Rule: Evaluated Logistic Regression, Random Forest, HistGradientBoosting, and XGBoost on 5-fold CV PR-AUC. Under the one-standard-error rule, {{ r.model_name }} is chosen as the simplest, most interpretable model within 1 SE of the top performer.

⚖️ Class-Imbalance Strategy Study

Ablation Study
Finding: Class-weighting and SMOTE artificially inflate false positives without improving ranking quality (PR-AUC). Keeping pure calibrated probabilities and tuning the decision threshold via business economics achieves superior net benefit.
